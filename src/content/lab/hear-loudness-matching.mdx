When two versions of a sound differ in level, the louder one almost always sounds better: fuller, brighter, more detailed. So a fair comparison has to match the loudness of both first, or it only tells you which one is louder.

This uses synthetic test audio: version B is pink noise with a 6 dB high shelf, played 8 LU louder. Turn "Match loudness" off and B wins by being louder; turn it on and you hear only the change in tone.
