On my Samsung Galaxy S21 FE running One UI 8.0, Apple Music only renders Dolby Atmos spatially when the system-level Dolby Atmos toggle is switched on. With the toggle off, the app still shows the Atmos badge, but the track plays back non-spatially. The badge describes the file, not what you’re actually hearing.

It shows the real problem: spatial audio on Android depends on each phone’s Dolby integration rather than on the app. That’s part of why I’m building [Stage](/projects/stage/), a spatial engine that lives inside the app itself.
