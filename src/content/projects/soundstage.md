title: SoundStage
tagline: Spatial audio for any song, on any phone.
summary: "Spatial audio today only works on Dolby Atmos devices, and only for songs mixed specially for it. SoundStage aims to change that. It separates a finished track into stems, then places them in space with genre-aware rules (an Afrobeat record shouldn’t be spatialised like house), staying true to the original mix and never rewriting it. Listening modes adapt to headphones, the car and different rooms."
