The limit is rarely the gear; it’s understanding, which is why I study the physics. And sound has a cost: our ears are the one piece of gear we can’t replace, yet the standard loudness meter reduces them to a single number. That gap is why I built [Beyond the Decibel](/projects/beyond-the-decibel/).
